# Реестр доказательств и гипотез

Дата: 2026-07-14

Задача: отделить science, programme precedent, product hypothesis, founder preference и unknowns.

## 1. Research-Supported

| Claim | Status | Product implication |
|---|---|---|
| Receptive-productive gap is real and multifaceted | Research-supported | diagnose and train recognition, recall, production separately |
| Productive vocabulary needs productive retrieval | Research-supported | keep productive SRS central |
| Retrieval and generation beat passive restudy for durable memory | Research-supported | every item needs recall, not only exposure |
| Spacing supports retention | Research-supported | keep SRS and delayed recycling |
| Contextualized vocabulary is stronger than isolated translation alone | Research-supported | tie words to context and use |
| Formulaic language reduces processing load | Research-supported/moderate | teach chunks, collocations, frames |
| Explicit grammar does not automatically become speech | Research-supported | train grammar in spoken/written production |
| Task repetition improves fluency and accuracy | Research-supported | repeat tasks with variation |
| Feedback works when matched to task and learner | Research-supported | prompt self-repair and retry |
| Prompts often support production better than recasts | Research-supported | keep prompt-based correction |
| Shadowing can support listening, prosody and fluency | Research-supported/moderate | use, but do not oversell |
| Extensive reading supports vocabulary/reading more than speaking | Research-supported | add output after reading |
| Foreign language anxiety affects performance | Research-supported | include safe exposure and state regulation |
| AI speaking practice may reduce anxiety short-term | Moderate/emerging | use AI rehearsal, test transfer |

## 2. Programme Precedent

| Pattern | Seen in | Product implication |
|---|---|---|
| Input → language focus → controlled practice → communicative task | coursebooks, live schools | structure units this way |
| Prompted recall before model | Pimsleur/Michel Thomas-like systems | use in phrase builder and productive SRS |
| Delayed active wave | Assimil-like systems | revisit earlier input productively |
| Sentence SRS | Glossika/Clozemaster-like systems | use sentence/chunk cards |
| Live teacher feedback and assessment | FSI/DLI/IEPs/Lingoda | add human checkpoints |
| Role-play and information gap | communicative teaching | core B1→B2 interaction tasks |
| External proficiency samples | institutional systems | add monthly and cohort assessment |

## 3. Product Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Why plausible | How to test |
|---|---|---|
| Intensive English can convert passive B1/B2 islands into speech better than generic apps | combines input, productive retrieval, output, feedback | 8-12 week cohort with speaking pre/post |
| AI + weekly human checkpoint transfers better than AI-only | humans add unpredictability and accountability | randomized pilot |
| Guardians reduce avoidance and improve completion | anxiety/perfectionism are real barriers | compare output attempt rates |
| Native-language translation + separate interface language improves trust | user need clearly observed | onboarding A/B and retention |
| Personal imported content increases transfer | relevance improves encoding | compare generic vs imported-content units |
| Productive SRS state labels improve motivation | honesty reduces false progress | comprehension of dashboard + retention |
| Role-mask lowers speaking shame | plausible affective distancing | compare role vs direct personal task |

## 4. Founder Preferences

These can shape brand and experience, but are not scientific proof:

- heroine journey;
- guardians: devaluation, intimidation, mockery, perfectionism;
- Petrusinsky as historical inspiration;
- beautiful motion-rich modern design;
- adult, non-infantilizing tone;
- dislike of childish gamification;
- culturally rich books/films;
- humane intensity.

## 5. Unknown or Weak

| Claim | Risk | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Ultra-fast word display directly accelerates acquisition | may only create familiarity | controlled comparison |
| 600+ words/day is sustainable for consumer app users | overload/dropout | pilot adherence and delayed recall |
| 2-3 exposures are enough for "learned" | likely too broad | item-state tracking |
| AI conversation transfers to human conversation | not proven long-term | human speaking pre/post |
| Native speakers will participate reliably | incentive/safety unknown | concierge pilot |
| Petrusinsky claims transfer directly to app | historical method not app-validated | small experiments |
| Baroque music improves language outcomes | weak as direct mechanism | treat only as atmosphere/state |
| Typing materially improves speaking | weak | keep for writing/form, not speech claim |

## 6. Marketing Red Lines

Do not claim:

- B1→C1 in 6 months as guaranteed;
- C2 in a year;
- AI replaces a teacher;
- streak equals progress;
- passive watching/reading teaches speaking;
- alpha/theta/binaural states cause language acquisition;
- native-like accent guaranteed;
- "method of diplomats" without explaining full-time institutional conditions.

Safe claim:

Intensive English trains the bridge from understanding to use: vocabulary, chunks, grammar-in-speech, pronunciation, output, feedback and real transfer.
