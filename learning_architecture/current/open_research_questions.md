# Open Research Questions

Date: 2026-07-14

Purpose: identify unresolved decisions before implementation tickets.

## 1. Method and Evidence

1. Which Petrusinsky mechanics have reliable primary sources beyond historical/promotional descriptions?
2. What is the safe and useful speed range for rapid word presentation in a consumer app?
3. How many items per daily block optimize retention without overload for A2/B1 adults?
4. Does "word flood" improve later recognition beyond normal preview with audio and translation?
5. Which claims about baroque music/readiness should be kept as atmosphere only?

## 2. Learning Architecture

1. What exact threshold creates a productive SRS card: recognition reps, context sightings, or both?
2. When does an item count as active: produced once, twice, after delay, or in transfer?
3. How many model examples are needed before personal sentence generation?
4. Which grammar constructions are highest priority for B1→B2 speech?
5. Should grammar progression follow CEFR, frequency, user errors, or communicative functions?

## 3. Assessment

1. What rubric will score speaking samples beyond self-ratings?
2. Should there be external human scoring in MVP?
3. How often can speaking assessment happen without causing anxiety?
4. What minimum evidence shows B1→B2 movement?
5. How to measure transfer from AI to human interaction?

## 4. Content

1. What content rights model is safest for imported books/films/videos?
2. How many chunks should be extracted per scene/chapter?
3. Which genres best serve adult women who freeze when speaking?
4. Should user-imported content become default or advanced mode?
5. How to keep films/books pleasurable while still training?

## 5. AI

1. Which ASR quality is acceptable for Russian-accented English?
2. Which feedback errors should AI detect first?
3. How to avoid overwhelming corrections?
4. How to store transcripts ethically?
5. What should be human-reviewed before trusting AI feedback?

## 6. Human and Community

1. What would native speakers receive: money, exchange, status, cultural contact?
2. How to train native speakers not to overcorrect?
3. What safety policy is needed for local/offline meetings?
4. What matching variables matter most: level, topic, gender preference, time zone, anxiety level?
5. What is the smallest viable coached group format?

## 7. Mobile/Web Split

1. Which tasks are currently duplicated across mobile/web without method reason?
2. What should web "Today" become if mobile completed the daily session?
3. How much grammar can be done on mobile before it becomes frustrating?
4. Should web become "studio" only after a mobile habit is established?
5. Which analytics belong on mobile without increasing shame?

## 8. Product Pilots

Recommended first pilots:

1. AI-only vs AI + weekly human checkpoint.
2. Generic content vs imported personal content.
3. Open "make a sentence" vs scaffolded sentence ladder.
4. Recognition SRS only vs recognition + productive SRS.
5. Role-mask output vs direct personal output.
6. 12-minute sustainable daily vs accelerated mode.

## 9. Decisions Needed From Founder

1. How central should Petrusinsky branding be versus evidence-based "bridge to speech" positioning?
2. Is the core promise B1→B2, speaking activation, or humane intensive habit?
3. Which audience first: immigrants, professionals, mothers, psychologists/coaches, or general adult women?
4. How much human service can the business support?
5. Is the first paid product app subscription, cohort, or hybrid?

## 10. Stop Condition

Do not build more screens until these are decided:

- active-word definition;
- core B1→B2 weekly loop;
- first pilot design;
- human checkpoint model;
- evidence-safe marketing promise.
