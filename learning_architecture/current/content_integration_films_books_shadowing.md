# Content Integration: Films, Books, Podcasts, Shadowing

Date: 2026-07-14

Principle: culture should not become a worksheet factory. Films, books and podcasts stay pleasurable, but the curriculum extracts a small amount of language and converts it into use.

## 1. Universal Content Loop

For every content source:

1. pre-task;
2. input;
3. comprehension;
4. language extraction;
5. phrase selection;
6. listening or reading focus;
7. pronunciation or prosody work;
8. retrieval;
9. discussion/retell;
10. writing;
11. real-life transfer;
12. later recycling.

The system should extract little and reuse deeply.

## 2. Film Scene Flow

Pre-task:

- show title, situation, characters;
- predict what happens;
- pre-teach 3-5 blocking words only.

Input:

- watch once for gist;
- watch selected 1-3 minute scene again.

Comprehension:

- choose gist;
- answer 2 detail questions.

Extraction:

- 3 useful chunks;
- 1 repair phrase or emotional phrase;
- 1 pronunciation/rhythm line.

Practice:

- shadow one line;
- reconstruct one line from audio;
- role-play same scene with changed detail.

Output:

- retell what happened in 4 sentences;
- answer: "What would you say in this situation?"

Recycling:

- chunks go to productive SRS;
- one scene phrase appears in later AI/human scenario.

## 3. Series Episode Flow

Pre-task:

- recap characters;
- choose one focus: relationship, conflict, work, humor.

Input:

- watch episode or selected 8-12 minute segment.

Learning:

- do not extract too much;
- choose 5 recurring phrases across the episode;
- identify one discourse move: disagree, soften, joke, apologize.

Output:

- 60-second episode recap;
- opinion with reason;
- role-play missing scene.

Transfer:

- use one phrase in personal update or club discussion.

## 4. Book Chapter Flow

Pre-task:

- read title/context;
- pre-teach 5 words if needed.

Input:

- read chapter/fragment for meaning.

Comprehension:

- summarize in L1 or English depending on level;
- identify who wants what.

Extraction:

- 5 phrases, not isolated rare words;
- 1 sentence pattern worth copying.

Practice:

- sentence imitation;
- short written response;
- oral retell with notes.

Transfer:

- book club question;
- compare to own life.

Recycling:

- phrase cards and later discussion prompts.

## 5. Short Story Flow

Best for A2-B1 because it is complete and compact.

Sequence:

- read/listen;
- retell beginning-middle-end;
- extract 3 chunks;
- transform one sentence into own life;
- record 45-second retell;
- later retell faster.

## 6. Podcast Flow

Pre-task:

- topic prediction;
- vocabulary pulse check.

Input:

- listen first without transcript;
- listen again with transcript.

Comprehension:

- gist;
- speaker stance;
- 3 key ideas.

Language:

- discourse markers;
- opinion frames;
- natural reductions.

Output:

- "I agree/disagree because...";
- 60-second voice note;
- one follow-up question for discussion.

## 7. Real Conversation Flow

Before:

- goal;
- 5 chunks;
- repair phrases;
- anxiety plan.

During:

- communicate, not perform;
- use at least 2 target moves;
- ask for clarification once.

After:

- save 3 phrases heard;
- save 1 mistake to repair;
- repeat improved version;
- schedule one phrase.

## 8. Shadowing Flow

Shadowing is not the whole speaking curriculum.

Use it for:

- prosody;
- connected speech;
- mouth confidence;
- phrase speed.

Sequence:

1. understand line;
2. hear line;
3. read with stress marks;
4. repeat after;
5. speak together;
6. speak alone;
7. record;
8. use line as a frame for own sentence.

## 9. Success Metrics

For content blocks, track:

- completion of input;
- comprehension accuracy;
- chunks extracted;
- chunks recalled later;
- spoken/written output created;
- one transfer use;
- learner enjoyment rating.

Enjoyment matters. But without retrieval and output, content remains exposure, not complete training.
