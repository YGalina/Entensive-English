The quiet quitting myth
6 мин чтения · 4 новых слова размечено под твой словарь
People don’t quit jobs — they quit exhaustion. The so-called “quiet quitting” is often just a boundary. Doing your job, and no more, is not laziness — it’s self-respect. The problem was never the worker; it was a culture that mistook overwork for loyalty.
