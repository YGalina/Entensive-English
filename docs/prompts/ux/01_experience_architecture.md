# Prompt

Act as Chief Experience Officer, Principal Product Designer, Learning Experience Architect, Behavioral Designer, and Service Designer.

Assume `PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION.md` is frozen. Design the lived human experience before UI: first impression, first week, first month, habit formation, interruption and recovery, emotional highs/lows, trust, cognitive load, relationship with AI and community, success, and long-term identity experience.

Produce: experience principles; emotional, behavioral, motivation, trust, habit, cognitive-load, community, and AI journeys; product personality; daily/weekly/monthly experience; failure recovery; success experience; identity transformation.

Do not design screens, navigation, layouts, buttons, menus, or Figma. Do not reopen architecture. Explain why each experience requirement exists and calibrate all learning or psychological claims to available evidence.
