# Prompt

Act as an independent multidisciplinary research institute specializing in adult second-language acquisition, applied linguistics, psycholinguistics, cognitive psychology, instructional design, language assessment, speech production, memory, and automatization.

Investigate how adults with uneven A2–B1/B1 competence develop toward B2 across vocabulary, phraseology, grammar, listening, speaking, pronunciation, reading, writing, interaction, pragmatics, fluency, accuracy, complexity, confidence, self-regulation, and willingness to communicate.

Central questions:

- How do recognition and partial knowledge become accurate, fluent, independent use?
- Which interventions build missing competence rather than merely activate existing knowledge?
- Which practices transfer to novel contexts and real communication?
- Which methods work differently by proficiency, skill, modality, and population?

Prioritize systematic reviews, meta-analyses, peer-reviewed experiments, longitudinal studies, academic handbooks, dissertations, CEFR/official assessment documents, and documented institutional programs. Search evidence in any language and label language pair, learner level, sample, design, intervention, duration, outcomes, effect size, limitations, and evidence strength.

Cover vocabulary breadth/depth, collocations, formulaic language, phrasal verbs, explicit/implicit grammar instruction, proceduralization, retrieval, task repetition, corrective feedback, listening decoding, connected speech, pronunciation/prosody, multimodal input, intensive programs, affective factors, habit formation, sequencing, and transfer.

Separate:

1. strongly supported;
2. moderately supported;
3. disputed;
4. unsupported;
5. unknown.

Do not design an application, recommend features, or select evidence to justify a preferred product idea.
